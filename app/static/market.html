What the research found

In IMPI’s 2015–2022 data (exploratory, not pre-registered): daily Nifty 500 moves came before changes in FII stock-futures positioning — FIIs chased price. Changes in FII index-futures positioning had some lead on index returns. Days when FII stock-futures positioning sat at the bottom of its 52-week range were followed by above-average 60-day index returns — a contrarian pattern. None of this improved stock-level breakout predictions.

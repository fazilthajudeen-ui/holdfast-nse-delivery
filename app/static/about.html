About the project

Built as a research project.

A research project testing whether NSE’s daily delivery data can tell lasting price rises from fading ones — turned into a free, plain-English tool anyone can use.

01

Plain words

Every number has a one-line meaning. Tap any ? to learn it.

02

Honest results

Tests were written down before they were run. Failures are published next to wins.

03

Free data

Everything comes from official NSE files, refreshed every trading evening at 7:30 PM.

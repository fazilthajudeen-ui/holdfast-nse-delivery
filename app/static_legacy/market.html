What the research found

In IMPI's 2015–2022 data (exploratory analysis, not a pre-registered result): daily Nifty 500 moves strongly preceded changes in FII stock-futures positioning (FIIs chased price), while changes in FII index-futures positioning had some lead on index returns. Days when FII stock-futures positioning sat at the bottom of its 52-week range were followed by above-average 60-day index returns — a contrarian pattern. None of this was strong enough to improve stock-level breakout predictions. Research tool — not investment advice.

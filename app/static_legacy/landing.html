How it works

One score. Four honest steps.

Every stock is judged against its own history, never a fixed rule. Only past data is used.

01

Delivery

Share of today’s traded shares actually taken home by buyers.

02

Normal

That stock’s usual delivery: the median of its previous 60 days.

03

Gap

How far above or below normal it has been, on a 5-day average.

04

Score

Ranked 0–100 against the stock’s own last 250 days. 70+ is High.

About the project

Built as a research project.

A research project testing whether NSE’s daily delivery data tells lasting price rises from fading ones, turned into a live terminal.

Project
Delivery-Based Movement Analysis on NSE
Data
NSE daily bhavcopy with delivery, Nifty 500 stocks, updated every trading evening

Research and education tool. Not investment advice. One year of data is a pilot study: results are indicative, not proof. Delivery shows that shares were taken, not who took them or why.

Research

IMPI tested whether delivery behaviour, futures positioning and price compression — and their interactions — predict breakout success on NSE, using official NSE data from 2014 to 2026, hypotheses committed to git before any test, and a test set (2023 onwards) evaluated exactly once.

Models in use — improved after the pre-registered study (post-hoc)

v2 was trained on every F&O stock-day with extra features and cross-sectional ranking. v3 adds option-implied volatility from the F&O bhavcopy and 20-day targets, under a protocol pre-registered on 11 Oct 2026 (impi/pre_registered_v3.md); a v3 model replaces v2 only if its CV AUC is ≥ 0.005 higher in ≥ 4 of 5 folds. All models were chosen only by walk-forward cross-validation on 2015–2022. Test-period numbers are post-hoc: that period had already been used once for the pre-registered study. v4 — re-framed hypotheses (pre-registered 12 Oct 2026)

Delivery is direction-neutral: every delivered share has a seller, so high delivery shows conviction, not who is in control. v4 tests delivery as a predictor of move size (D1) and, signed by price direction, of direction (D2); re-tests the price-up/OI-down lead on all breakouts (H2′); tests positioning the way Larry Williams used the COT report (C1); and a looser VCP rule (V1). Rules were fixed in git before testing (impi/pre_registered_v4.md). The 2023–26 check below ran once on a previously used period; the live forward record is the confirmatory test.

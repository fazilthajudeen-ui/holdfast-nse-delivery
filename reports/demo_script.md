# Booth demo script (≈ 3 minutes)

1. **Hook (Overview page):** "When a stock jumps 20%, NSE tells us how many shares buyers actually *took home*. We asked: does that predict whether the jump sticks?"
2. **Play (Demo: Guess the Stock):** hand the visitor the mouse: "Two real stocks, same size rally. Which one held its gains?" Let them guess, press *Reveal*, and read out each stock's delivery vs its own normal.
3. **Evidence (Historical Analysis):** point at the dark-blue bars: "Across 656 rallies, high-delivery moves beat low-delivery ones by 1–2 points over the next weeks, significantly, and gave back half their rise less often (49% vs 59%). At 3 months the gap is bigger but not yet proven."
4. **Live (Stock Explorer → Leaderboard):** type the visitor's favourite stock, show today's Conviction Score gauge, then the "Up > 10% on low conviction" watch list, refreshed every evening at 7:30 PM from NSE.
5. **Honesty close:** "One year is a pilot: delivery shows shares were taken, not who took them or why. This is research, not investment advice."
